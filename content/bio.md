I am a PhD student at [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/), working in the **State Key Laboratory of Information Engineering in Surveying, Mapping and Remote Sensing**. I expect to graduate in June 2027.

My research focuses on **multimodal satellite image matching** and **geometric processing of remote sensing imagery**, including robust feature representation, cross-modal correspondence learning, and high-precision image registration.

I am the first author of **MIRRIFT** and **FRIM**, two studies published in *IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing* in 2025. I also work on research software and intellectual-property outputs related to satellite remote sensing image matching and image processing.

Previously, I received my Master's degree in Resources and Environment (Surveying Engineering) from Wuhan University and my Bachelor's degree in Surveying Engineering from Taiyuan University of Technology.
